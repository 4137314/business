#+title: Numerical practice and worked answers
#+summary: Calculate, state the convention and interpret the result; a correct number without the right meaning is incomplete. Exercises follow the course's numerical cases.

* Problems
1. Vendor grid: weights quality 0.6, delivery 0.4. A scores (5,2), B (3,4).
   Rank them, then repeat with equal weights.
2. TCO: machine A costs 8,000 + 3,000 per year for three years; B costs 11,000
   + 1,500 per year. Ignore discounting. Which has lower TCO?
3. Depreciation: cost 18,000, residual 3,000, five years. Annual charge and
   carrying amount after two years.
4. Liquidity: cash 15, receivables 35, inventory 50, current liabilities 80.
   NWC, treasury margin, acid test.
5. ROI: sales 800, EBIT 80, operating IC 400. ROS, ROI, turnover.
6. CPM: A=2, B=5 after A, C=3 after A, D=2 after B and C. Duration, critical
   path, float of C; then C = 6.
7. Earned value: PV=60, EV=48, AC=54. CV, SV, CPI, SPI.
8. Purchasing leverage: sales 10,000, materials 5,000, other costs 4,500,
   total assets 8,000 of which inventory 2,000 (half materials). Compare ROA
   after (a) sales +10% with all costs +10%; (b) materials −4%.
9. ABC: spends 50, 20, 10, 8, 5, 3, 2, 1, 0.6, 0.4 (EUR thousand). Classify.
10. GE weighting: three factors with weights 0.5/0.3/0.2 scored 4/2/5. Score?
11. Break-even: price 40, variable cost 25, fixed costs 60,000. Q*, and Q* if
    price drops to 35.
12. Capability: tolerance 40–160 s, σ = 25 s. Cp and judgement.
13. Process efficiency: activities 4 (NVA), 10 (VA), 6 (NVA), 20 (NVA), 5 (VA).
14. Kanban: demand 300/day, lead time 0.4 day, safety 25%, container 25.
15. Inventata sas: after founding (20,000), loan (5,000), vehicle (10,000 cash),
    materials 2,000 cash, sale of goods costing 300 for 500 on credit — total
    assets and equity?
16. ALFA: Li 78,228; Ld 942,605; AC 2,249,823; AI 232,458; Pcor 1,363,449;
    Pcon 472,746; PN 646,086. MT, CCN, IL, ISP, IS; would you lend for 120 days?

* Worked answers
1. A = 0.6·5 + 0.4·2 = 3.8; B = 0.6·3 + 0.4·4 = 3.4 → A. Equal weights: 3.5 each.
2. A = 8,000 + 9,000 = 17,000; B = 11,000 + 4,500 = 15,500 → B, although A is
   cheaper to buy.
3. (18,000 − 3,000)/5 = 3,000; after two years 12,000.
4. CA = 100; NWC = 20; MT = 15 + 35 − 80 = −30; acid test = 50/80 = 0.625.
5. ROS 10%, ROI 20%, turnover 2 (10% × 2 = 20%).
6. A–B–D = 9 days critical, C float 2; with C = 6, A–C–D = 10 is critical and
   B gets 1 day of float.
7. CV = −6; SV = −12; CPI = 0.889; SPI = 0.80: over budget and late.
8. Base: margin 500, ROS 5%, turnover 1.25, ROA 6.25%. (a) margin 550, sales
   11,000, ROS 5%, turnover 1.375, ROA 6.88%. (b) materials −200 → margin 700,
   inventory −40 → assets 7,960, ROS 7%, ROA 8.79%. Leverage 1/ROS = 20: one
   euro saved equals twenty euros of extra sales.
9. Total 100. Cumulative: 50, 70, 80 → A (3 suppliers, 30%); 88, 93 → B; the
   rest C (7% of spend, 50% of suppliers).
10. 0.5·4 + 0.3·2 + 0.2·5 = 3.6 (medium-high).
11. Q* = 60,000/15 = 4,000; at 35: 60,000/10 = 6,000 (+50% volume needed).
12. Cp = 120/150 = 0.8 < 1: not capable even if in control.
13. VA = 15 of 45 min → 33%.
14. N = 300 · 0.4 · 1.25 / 25 = 6 cards.
15. Assets: cash 13,000; stock 1,700; vehicle 10,000; receivable 500 = 25,200.
    Liabilities 5,000; equity 20,000 + profit 200 = 20,200.
16. MT = −342,616; CCN = 886,374; IL = (2,249,823 − 1,228,990)/1,363,449 = 0.75;
    ISP = 2.78; IS = 2.84. Liquidity weak and dependence critical, but the debt is
    bank debt granted after checks and receivables grew with sales: a 120-day
    loan is defensible with guarantees, after reading the notes.

* Pitfall
Label units and denominators. Money-based schedule variance is not a duration;
a ratio of 0.2 means 20%, while an increase from 10% to 20% is ten percentage
points. Always state the convention (e.g. which invested capital).
