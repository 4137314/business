#+title: Business Organization and Management: study map
#+summary: A connected study path from organizational choices to customers, suppliers, operations, financial performance and project delivery.

* Study method
Read foundations first, then follow prerequisites. For each concept, explain the
definition aloud, work through the example, identify a pitfall and answer the
recall question before looking back. Use lateral links to connect decisions
across functions. Revisit difficult concepts after a day and again after a week.

Use the revision exercises after the underlying concepts. They are original
practice material, not a prediction of the assessment or its marking scheme.

* Exam strategy
For an open question: (1) define the concept precisely with the author and
year; (2) draw the model or matrix with labelled axes; (3) apply it to the
course company example; (4) add a numerical check if the topic has a formula;
(5) close with a limitation or a link to another model (e.g. ABC → Kraljic,
five forces → value chain → RBV, quality → Six Sigma → Lean).
