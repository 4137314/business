#+title: Total cost of ownership
#+summary: TCO compares suppliers on all costs of buying, using and disposing of an item over its life — pre-transaction, transaction and post-transaction — not only the purchase price.

* Definition
TCO compares suppliers including *all costs associated with buying and using* a
good over its useful life (life-cycle analysis), not just the initial price:
financial, logistic and disposal costs; discounting can be applied.
| Stage            | Cost items                                           |
|------------------+------------------------------------------------------|
| Pre-transaction  | supplier analysis, qualification and evaluation      |
| Transaction      | purchase price, delivery (shipping, tariffs, taxes), |
|                  | inspection and audit                                 |
| Post-transaction | returns, downtime from incomplete or defective       |
|                  | deliveries, warranty                                 |
Everyday case: energy bills — the cheapest tariff per kWh is not always the
cheapest contract once fixed fees and conditions are included.

* Worked example
*Aluminium oxide*, 2,000 t from three suppliers (EUR):
| Item                        | A       | B       | C         |
|-----------------------------+---------+---------+-----------|
| Unit price (EUR/t)          | 80.00   | 82.60   | 84.20     |
| Initial cost                | 160,000 | 165,200 | 168,400   |
| Discount                    | (see *) | −5%     | −4%       |
| Net initial cost            | 152,000 | 156,940 | 161,664   |
| Purification 50 EUR/t on    | 6% →    | 4% →    | 3% →      |
| impure share                | 6,000   | 4,000   | 3,000     |
| Financial cost (pay 30 days | 1,520   | 1,569   | 0         |
| early at 12%/yr = 1%)       |         |         |           |
| Transport                   | 17,817  | 15,493  | 16,526    |
| Insurance                   | 2,065   | 2,065   | 2,065     |
| Handling                    | 1,032   | 1,032   | 1,032     |
| Duties                      | 619     | 413     | 0         |
| Total logistics             | 21,533  | 19,003  | 19,623    |
| Total costs                 | 181,053 | 181,512 | 184,287   |
| Impurity quality factor     | 105%    | 102%    | 100%      |
| *TCO*                       | 190,106 | 185,142 | *184,287* |
(*) A's quoted discount is 6%, but the net cost 152,000 corresponds to 5%;
with 6% it would be 150,400 and TCO 188,409 — C still wins.
Standard terms: 90 days from invoice; A and B require payment at 60 days, i.e.
30 days early: \( 152{,}000 \times 12\% \times 30/360 = 1{,}520 \).
*A is cheapest on price and on total costs, but C has the lowest TCO* once the
quality penalty for impurities is included.

* Pitfall
TCO is not the price plus freight: quality, downtime, payment terms and
disposal are part of it. Factors like the 105% quality coefficient are
estimates — state them.

* Recall
List pre-, transaction and post-transaction costs; reproduce the aluminium
oxide table and explain why C wins.
