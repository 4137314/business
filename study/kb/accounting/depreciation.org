#+title: Depreciation and carrying amount
#+summary: Depreciation spreads the cost of a fixed asset over its useful life; with the straight-line method the annual charge is (cost − residual value)/life and the carrying amount falls by that charge every year.

* Model
Fixed assets live longer than one year. Instead of charging the whole cost in
the purchase year, depreciation spreads it over the useful life, reflecting
wear and use.
\[ Q = \frac{V_{\text{initial}} - V_{\text{residual}}}{n}, \qquad
   V_k = V_{\text{initial}} - \sum_{j=1}^{k} Q_j = V_{\text{initial}} - kQ \]
\( Q \) annual depreciation (/quota di ammortamento/), \( n \) useful life in
years, \( V_k \) carrying amount after \( k \) years. Assumes full years, no
impairment or revaluation, unchanged estimates.

* Worked example
*10 laptops*, total EUR 10,000, life 5 years, residual 0:
\( Q = (10{,}000 - 0)/5 = 2{,}000 \) per year.
| Year           | 0      | 1     | 2     | 3     | 4     | 5     |
|----------------+--------+-------+-------+-------+-------+-------|
| Depreciation   | –      | 2,000 | 2,000 | 2,000 | 2,000 | 2,000 |
| Carrying value | 10,000 | 8,000 | 6,000 | 4,000 | 2,000 | 0     |
*Car*: cost 20,000, residual 2,000, life 10 years →
\( Q = (20{,}000 - 2{,}000)/10 = 1{,}800 \); after 5 years
\( V_5 = 20{,}000 - 5 \times 1{,}800 = 11{,}000 \).
*Machine*: cost 12,000, residual 2,000, 5 years → 2,000 per year; after three
years 6,000.

* Intuition
The asset is a resource consumed over several periods: depreciation matches its
consumption with the revenues it helps generate ([[id:financial-statements][accrual principle]]).
Each charge lowers the year's profit and the asset's carrying amount, but no
cash moves.

* Pitfall
Depreciation is not a cash reserve nor a market value. Do not go below the
residual value. The purchase payment (cash) and depreciation (cost) are
separate events.

* Recall
Write both formulas; rebuild the laptop table; compute the car's value after
5 years; explain why depreciation reduces profit but not cash.
